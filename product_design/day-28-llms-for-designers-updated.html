Deep Understanding of LLMs

How LLMs Actually Work

An LLM is a Transformer neural network trained on massive text. It doesn't "understand" — it predicts the most statistically likely next word, billions of times.

What LLMs Actually Do

Input → Tokenize → Transformer layers → Output

Trained on trillions of tokens: books, web, code

Parameters = the "memory" of the model (billions)

No real reasoning — pure statistical prediction

Tokens, Probability & Prediction

A token ≈ ¾ of a word. The model assigns probability scores to every possible next token — then picks one based on temperature.

What LLMs Actually Do

1 token ≈ ¾ word — AI charges per token (real cost)

Context window = AI's working memory limit

Temperature 0 = deterministic; 1.0+ = creative

Probability, not logic, drives every word choice

Why AI Gives Different Answers Each Time

Temperature controls randomness. Low = classical musician playing sheet music exactly. High = jazz musician improvising every time.

UX Design Implications

Hallucination & Weaknesses

AI invents plausible-sounding facts with complete confidence. It doesn't know what it doesn't know — it just generates the most statistically likely text.

ChatGPT · chat.openai.com

User: Does AI hallucinate real medical studies?

AI: Yes — for example, the JAMA 2019 study by Johnson et al. showed a 47% reduction in patient errors across 12,400 participants when using AI-assisted diagnosis.

⚠ This study does not exist. AI fabricated all three red-highlighted facts with complete confidence.

What LLMs Actually Do

Hallucination: AI invents plausible facts confidently

Knowledge cutoff: the world stops at the training date

Context limit: AI forgets early parts of long chats

Add source citations + human review gate always

Why AI Sounds Confident But Is Wrong

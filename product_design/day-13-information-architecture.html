01 Information Architecture (IA)

📌
02 / 28

WHAT IT IS ?

The way content, features, and information are structured so users can easily navigate and find what they need.

📌
03 / 28

HOW IT WORKS ?

Content is grouped into meaningful categories.

Information is organised in a logical hierarchy (broad → specific).

Users move through the structure using search or navigation.

📌
04 / 28

WHY IT MATTERS ?

When information is hard to find, users feel lost and leave quickly.

Insight: Keep hierarchy shallow (2–3 levels). Deep structures increase effort and confusion.

02 Mental Models

📌
05 / 28

WHAT IT IS ?

The assumptions and expectations users bring based on their past experiences with other products.

📌
06 / 28

HOW IT WORKS ?

Users expect familiar patterns (cart, back button, swipe actions).

They predict how things should behave before interacting.

Mismatches between expectation and behavior cause friction.

📌
07 / 28

WHY IT MATTERS ?

Design feels "easy" when it behaves the way users expect.

💡 Insight: Users don't learn your interface from scratch — they compare it with what they already know.

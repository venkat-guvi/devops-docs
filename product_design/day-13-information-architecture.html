01 Information Architecture (IA)

WHAT IT IS ?The way content, features, and information are structured so users can easily navigate and find what they need.HOW IT WORKS ?Content is grouped into meaningful categories. Information is organised in a logical hierarchy (broad → specific).Users move through the structure using search or navigation.WHY IT MATTERS ?When information is hard to find, users feel lost and leave quickly.
Insight: Keep hierarchy shallow (2–3 levels). Deep structures increase effort and confusion.

02 Mental Models

WHAT IT IS ?The assumptions and expectations users bring based on their past experiences with other products.HOW IT WORKS ?Users expect familiar patterns (cart, back button, swipe actions). They predict how things should behave before interacting. Mismatches between expectation and behavior cause friction.WHY IT MATTERS ?Design feels "easy" when it behaves the way users expect.
💡 Insight: Users don't learn your interface from scratch — they compare it with what they already know.

03 Content Structuring

WHAT IT IS ?Arranging content to guide attention.HOW IT WORKS ?Show important items first, group related content.WHY IT MATTERS ?Users scan quickly. Less content = better decisions.
💡 Insight: Less content = better decisions.

04 Sitemap & Navigation

WHAT IT IS ? Structure of screens + movement between them.HOW IT WORKS ?Sitemap = hierarchy. Navigation = flow.WHY IT MATTERS ?Users need direction. Clear structure + simple movement.
💡 Insight: Clear structure + simple movement keeps users oriented at every step.

05 Card Sorting

WHAT IT IS ?Understanding how users group information.HOW IT WORKS ?Users create or choose categories.WHY IT MATTERS ?Aligns design with user thinking.
💡 Insight: Users think in use-cases, not systems.

06 UX Audits

WHAT IT IS ?Evaluating UX using standard principles.HOW IT WORKS ?Identify issues and assign severity.WHY IT MATTERS ?Fix problems early.
💡 Insight: Missing feedback is the most common audit finding.

07 Usability Issues

WHAT IT IS ?Friction in user experience.HOW IT WORKS ?Identify slow or confusing steps.WHY IT MATTERS ?Impacts conversion. Small issues = big drop-offs.
💡 Insight: Small usability issues = big drop-offs in conversion.

08 End-to-End User Journey

WHAT IT IS ?Full user flow analysis.HOW IT WORKS ?Map steps and identify pain points.WHY IT MATTERS ?One issue affects the entire journey.
💡 Insight: Small improvements = big impact on the full journey.
